Find Your Protocol

Common protocol structures, organized by goal — based on how these peptides and stacks are typically used. This is a starting reference, not a prescription; your actual protocol is set during intake with a licensed provider.

How to use this page: each card below describes a protocol structure and the rationale behind it — not doses, frequencies, or self-administration instructions. Specific dosing is determined by your provider based on your intake.
Weight & metabolic goals

Weight Loss & Metabolic Protocols

GLP-1s drive the largest total weight-loss numbers; GH-pathway peptides shift the emphasis toward visceral fat and muscle preservation.

Maximum Overall Weight Loss

Semaglutide or Tirzepatide (Primary)

GLP-1 receptor agonists (and dual GLP-1/GIP agonists) deliver the largest total weight-loss effect of anything in this category. Adding other peptides on top won't meaningfully increase the scale number.

Best for: Individuals whose primary goal is total weight loss, with muscle preservation as a secondary concern.

Weight Loss + Muscle Preservation

GLP-1 + Ipamorelin (or CJC-1295/Ipamorelin)

The GLP-1 drives the deficit and the weight loss; the GH secretagogue's protein-sparing, anabolic effect helps offset the lean-mass loss that typically accompanies a GLP-1-driven deficit.

Best for: Anyone on a GLP-1 who wants to protect muscle mass during the loss phase — resistance training remains the single biggest lever for this goal.
Discuss This Protocol
Visceral Fat Loss + Muscle Preservation

Tesamorelin (± Ipamorelin)

Tesamorelin's approved effect is specifically visceral fat reduction, and the GH-pathway mechanism is inherently protein-sparing rather than deficit-driven — it doesn't carry the same lean-mass risk as a GLP-1-led approach.

Best for: Clients whose primary goal is visceral fat and body composition rather than a large total-weight-loss number.
Discuss This Protocol
Visceral Fat Loss + Muscle Gain

Tesamorelin or CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + BPC-157/TB-500

GH-pathway peptides supply the anabolic/lipolytic environment; the repair peptides support recovery from the added resistance training this goal actually requires — meaningful muscle gain still needs a training stimulus.

Best for: Clients pairing this protocol with an active resistance-training program, not as a standalone muscle-building approach.

Recovery & performance

Recovery Protocols

Built around soft-tissue repair and the training load that goes with an active lifestyle.

Soft-Tissue Recovery

Recovery Stack — BPC-157 + TB-500

BPC-157's localized tissue-repair effects paired with TB-500's systemic anti-inflammatory and cell-migration effects — the most common pairing for tendon, ligament, and muscle-strain recovery.

Best for: Active individuals managing a specific injury or recurring strain, alongside standard rehab.

Skin & Structural Recovery

GLOW / KLOW — GHK-Cu + BPC-157 + TB-500

Copper peptide for collagen/skin support paired with BPC-157 and TB-500 for underlying tissue repair. KLOW adds KPV for a more intensive protocol.

Best for: Clients focused on both visible skin quality and underlying tissue recovery in one protocol.

Longevity & cellular health

Anti-Aging & Cellular Protocols

These target aging at the cellular/mitochondrial level — positioned as foundational, ongoing protocols rather than single-symptom fixes.

Cellular Energy

NAD+ + MOTS-c

NAD+ replenishes the coenzyme pool needed for mitochondrial ATP production and DNA repair; MOTS-c supports mitochondrial signaling and metabolic resilience under stress.

Best for: Clients whose primary complaint is energy, mental clarity, or general cellular support.

Telomere & Longevity

Epithalon + NAD+

Epithalon is studied for potential telomerase-activity and circadian-regulation effects; NAD+ addresses the separate coenzyme-decline pathway of aging.

Best for: Clients interested in a foundational, longer-horizon longevity protocol.

Comprehensive Anti-Aging

NAD+ + Epithalon + GHK-Cu

A three-part protocol combining coenzyme restoration, telomere/circadian research, and structural/collagen support — often positioned as a bundled foundational offering.

Best for: Clients who want the broadest cellular-aging protocol in one package rather than three separate purchases.
