Frequently asked

Questions worth asking before you start.

Is this legal?

Products are sourced through Stryker Pharmacy (503A) and licensed 503B outsourcing facilities. Licensing documentation is available on request — we'd encourage you to verify it independently rather than take our word for it.

Do I need a prescription?

Most products require a licensed provider's review before dispensing. Our intake process connects you with a provider who evaluates candidacy — this isn't a self-checkout process.

How do I know the sourcing is safe?

Every batch is tied to a Certificate of Analysis (COA) confirming identity and purity. Ask for the COA on any product before use — any legitimate supplier should provide one.

I'm new to peptides — where do I start?

Start with the intake, not a product. A licensed provider reviewing your goals will point you to the right starting protocol rather than you having to guess from a catalog.

What does a MedSpa partnership actually involve?

A short partner call to review tiers and fit, a 60–90 day pilot with 2–3 SKUs, and staff-ready materials so your team isn't improvising the pitch to clients.

What does a physician need to get started?

Verifiable pharmacy licensing and relationship documentation, plus clinical reference materials for your team. No compliance infrastructure to build from scratch on your end.
